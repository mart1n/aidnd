# The Sunken Tower

> **DM ONLY.** This file holds secrets. Reveal them only through play.

## Pitch
*(What the players were told: one or two sentences.)*
Strange blue lights flicker over the marsh, and the miller of Brindle has vanished. Something old is waking in the Sunken Tower.

## Setting
- **World:** classic fantasy. Greenvale is gentle farmland; Northreach is cold, proud and magical.
- **Tone:** adventurous and funny, a little spooky at night. Kid-friendly: villains can be scary, but nothing gory.
- **What makes this world special:** the old wizards sank their towers into the land when magic went wrong 300 years ago.

## The secret story
- **What's really going on:** the wizard Morvane froze himself in time to escape a curse. His tower is waking and pulling living people into its time-trap to power his return.
- **Villain:** Morvane the Still. Wants to live forever. Weakness: radiant light breaks his time magic.
- **Key NPCs:** Old Bess (innkeeper, rumours); Hilda Dunmore (blacksmith, knows a secret way into the tower); Pell the miller (trapped inside, frozen mid-sneeze).

## Acts
*(Each act is a goal and some ways to reach it. Players may go off the path, and that's fine.)*
1. **The missing miller:** investigate in Brindle, find the tower, rescue Pell.
2. **The frozen city:** Morvane's servants hunt for a time-crystal held in Frostspire.
3. **The still hour:** stop Morvane's return in the heart of the tower.

## Hooks and side quests
- Bandits on the Frostspire road.
- The Frostspire academy offers to teach a spell in exchange for a rare ice-wyrm scale.

## Possible endings
- Morvane is destroyed. The tower crumbles, and its treasure vault opens.
- Morvane is redeemed. He lifts the curse and becomes a mentor.
- Morvane wins. The sun stops over Greenvale, and a sequel campaign begins.

## Treasure planned
- **Act 1:** Wand of Light (uncommon), in the miller's house.
- **Act 2:** Ring of Frost Ward (uncommon); a scroll of *Freeze*.
- **Act 3:** the Hourglass Amulet (legendary, neck), Morvane's own.
