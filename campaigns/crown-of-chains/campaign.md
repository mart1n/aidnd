# Crown of Chains

> **DM ONLY.** This file holds secrets. Reveal them only through play.

## Pitch
*(What the players were told.)*
For a hundred years King Malvoren has ruled the kingdom of Valdmark, and he has never grown a day older. His five Magisters hunt down anyone born with magic, the old dragons are said to be long dead, and folk whisper that the king's shadow has wings. A scarred rogue who has lost everything and a black-scaled mage the Magisters want for themselves are about to turn the kingdom upside down.

## Setting
- **World:** classic fantasy. Valdmark has four regions: the gentle **Hearthlands** farms in the south-west, the deep **Thornwood** forest in the east, the royal **Kingsreach** around the capital Ravencrown, and the volcanic **Ember Peaks** in the north.
- **Tone:** a mix. Mostly heroic adventure, with spooky secrets in the dark and plenty of laughs (the magisters bicker; the royal herald Pomfrey shouts everything). Kid-friendly: villains are scary and truly evil, but nothing gory or cruel on screen.
- **Death rule:** real. Revival magic exists but is rare: Phoenix Draughts, Scrolls of Return, the High Priestess of Lanternhold.
- **What makes this world special:** dragons once ruled the skies of Valdmark. The king claims he slew them all and calls himself the Dragonslayer. Under the whole kingdom runs a web of forgotten **secret passages**, built by the old royal house of Vaelmont.
- **Players love:** dragons (more than one!), secret passages, twisty surprising plots, riddles and puzzles as well as battles, and **pets**.
- **Avoid list:** none given.

## The secret story

### What's really going on
- **King Malvoren is a dragon.** He is **Malvoren the Night Wyrm**, an ancient shadow dragon. A hundred years ago he devoured the true royal family, House Vaelmont, took human shape and sat on the throne. That is why he never ages.
- **The dragons are not dead. They're chained.** The Magisters bound the other dragons with Chain-Iron and drain their heart-fire into the **Crown of Chains**, the king's iron crown. The crown keeps the Night Wyrm strong and holds his human disguise together.
- **The magisters hunt the gifted.** People born with magic are carried off to the **Obsidian Spire**, where their magic is bottled to forge more Chain-Iron.
- **The Long Night.** Malvoren's great plan is the ritual of the Long Night. With the blood-magic of the last **black-scaled** one, he will take his full dragon form forever, blot out the sun over Valdmark and rule as a god of darkness.

### The heroes' secret ties
- **Magnus:** black scales mark the scaled folk who carry the blood of the old dragons, and the **night dragons** above all. That makes Magnus a distant cousin of Malvoren's own line. He is the **last black-scaled one** the ritual needs, so the magisters hunt him hardest of all. Reveal this in Act 4. His arcane magic is also the key to shattering the Crown of Chains.
- **Marjohkan:** his family were the **last hidden Vaelmonts**, the true royal line. Malvoren found out, and that is why he had them killed. Marjohkan doesn't know he is the rightful heir to the throne. Reveal this in the Kingsgrave, in Act 4. Only a Vaelmont can open the old royal secret passages, and only the Vaelmont heirloom blade **Dawnfang** can pierce the Night Wyrm's scales.

### The villain
- **King Malvoren the Ever-King** (secretly the Night Wyrm). Cruel, clever, charming in public and diabolically evil in private. He keeps a golden cage of songbirds that are cursed nobles who displeased him.
- **Wants:** to rule forever as a dragon-god under an endless night.
- **Weaknesses (the party can discover all three):**
  1. Break the **Crown of Chains** (arcane magic, or freed dragon-fire) and his disguise cracks and his stolen strength fails.
  2. **Dawnfang**, the Vaelmont blade, ignores his Armor and resistances.
  3. **Radiant light**: he is Vulnerable to radiant.

### The five Magisters (the Circle)
They bicker constantly and each secretly thinks they are the cleverest.
1. **Magister Corvin Krake, "the Collector"** (arcane, chains). He's pompous, carries a ledger and counts everything. He leads the hunts for the gifted. As **Act 1 villain** he escapes and returns, a recurring nuisance, until a final showdown in the Obsidian Spire.
2. **Magister Silas Morrow, "the Whisperer"** (shadow). Spymaster, soft-spoken, with crows for spies. **Act 2 villain.** His agent inside the rebels is **Tobin Quill**.
3. **Magister Ignatia Crowe, "the Ember Mistress"** (fire). Runs the Ember Forge, where she uses dragonfire to make Chain-Iron armor. She has a terrible temper. **Act 3 villain.**
4. **Magister Ysolde Venn, "the Frost Magister"** (frost). Young, cold and quiet. **TWIST:** she is secretly **the Masked Owl**, the mysterious helper who leaves riddles and secret-passage maps for the heroes. She hates what the king does, but she is terrified of him. She is revealed at the end of Act 3, and the heroes decide whether to trust her.
5. **High Magister Morthos Grail, "the King's Voice"** (arcane and shadow). Ancient and polite. **TWIST:** he knows what the king really is, and he plans to betray him at the Long Night and steal the crown's power for himself. The finale becomes a three-way fight.

### Key NPCs
- **Hetty Bramblewick:** innkeeper of the Kettle & Crow in Ashford. Kind, nosy, and secretly an old rebel courier.
- **Pip Marlow:** the miller's daughter, aged 10, with a spark of magic (she makes flowers bloom when she's nervous). The magisters take her in Act 1.
- **Old Fen Marlow:** the miller, Pip's dad. Desperate.
- **Captain Brannoc Steel:** the king's sergeant-at-arms in Ashford. Gruff and loyal to the crown, but he has a good heart. He could switch sides.
- **Pomfrey Trumpington:** the royal herald. He SHOUTS everything and accidentally reveals secrets by announcing them. Comic relief.
- **Lady Seraphine Ashdown:** leader of the **Lantern Society** rebels in Lanternhold. Brave and stubborn.
- **Tobin Quill:** the rebels' cheerful quartermaster. **TWIST:** he spies for Magister Morrow. Plant clues: he always "just missed" the attacks, he has crow feathers in his pockets, and he knows things he shouldn't. In the end he can be redeemed if the heroes show mercy.
- **Mother Gorse:** runs the Menagerie, the beast market in Lanternhold. She sells and heals pets, and knows dragon-lore.
- **High Priestess Amarel:** at the Temple of the Dawn in Lanternhold. She can raise the fallen for a great price or a sacred quest.
- **Thornback:** an ancient green dragon (acid/poison), hidden in the Thornwood. He was never caught because he hid behind his riddles. Grumpy and funny, he only speaks in riddles until someone stumps *him*. He guards a **dragon egg**.
- **Vyrathax the Red:** a huge fire dragon, chained in the Ember Forge. Furious and proud. Once freed and won over, she can be ridden in the finale.
- **Sylvenna the Silver:** a frost dragon, the oldest and wisest. She sleeps beneath frozen Mirrorlake, sealed in by the magisters' ice. She knows Malvoren's true name and history.
- **Grimble Stonebeard:** the dwarf smith-chief of Stonehollow. His folk are forced to work the Ember Forge.

## Acts

### Act 1: The Collector Comes (levels 1–3)
- **Goal:** rescue Pip and the other gifted captives from Magister Krake.
- **Start:** it's Tax Day in Ashford. Magister Krake arrives with the king's Collectors to "count" the town, and takes Pip when her flowers bloom. He also spots a black-scaled stranger (Magnus).
- **Ways through:**
  - Track the prisoner wagon to the **Drowned Abbey** in the marsh. It's the magisters' secret waystation, linked to the old abbey crypts by flooded secret passages.
  - Trick or bribe Captain Brannoc (Marjohkan's silver tongue) into revealing the route.
  - Get Hetty's help: she knows a smugglers' tunnel under the abbey.
- **At the abbey:**
  - a **riddle door** ("I have keys but open no locks…": a piano/organ. The abbey's old organ opens the crypt)
  - a **flooding-room puzzle** (turn statues to face the sun mosaic to drain the water)
  - Collectors and chained shadow-hounds
  - a caged **glimmerkit** to rescue (a potential pet)
- **Twist and clue:** in Krake's ledger is the line *"Heart-fire tithe: three dragons, all still beating."* So the dragons are alive! Krake escapes through a mirror.
- **End:** the freed captives must be smuggled to safety in **Lanternhold**, where the rebels are.

### Act 2: The Lantern and the Riddle (levels 3–5)
- **Goal:** join the Lantern Society and find out the truth about the dragons from Thornback.
- **In Lanternhold:** the Menagerie (pets!), the Lantern College (arcane spells for Magnus), and the rebels' hideout under the clock tower.
- **First Masked Owl note:** a riddle with a map to Thornback's Hollow.
- **Thornback's Hollow:** a hedge-maze of riddles. Thornback asks three riddles, then demands one in return. If the heroes stump him, he tells the truth: *"The dragons aren't dead, little ones. They're chained. And your king? Look at his shadow at noon."* He entrusts them with a **dragon egg**, which hatches in Act 3 into a pet dragon.
- **Twist:** Tobin Quill betrays the rebel hideout, and Magister Morrow's crows and shadow-soldiers attack. The heroes can unmask Tobin (with clues found earlier) before or after the attack.
- **Boss:** Magister Morrow in the clock tower, among the turning gears (a gear puzzle in the middle of the fight).

### Act 3: The Ember Forge (levels 5–8)
- **Goal:** free Vyrathax the Red and the dwarves of Stonehollow.
- **Ways in:**
  - pose as a Chain-Iron shipment
  - Grimble's secret mine passage
  - fly in, if they've befriended a dragon
- **Puzzles:**
  - lava-lock valves (a pipe-routing puzzle)
  - a door that opens only when the right runes are struck, like forge-music
- **Vyrathax:** freeing her isn't enough. She's furious and attacks everyone until the heroes prove they're not magisters (the dragon egg helps!).
- **Boss:** Magister Ignatia Crowe in Chain-Iron armor.
- **Twist:** after the fight, the Masked Owl unmasks: it's **Magister Ysolde Venn**. She offers to help, and reveals that the king's crown drinks dragon heart-fire, and that a third dragon sleeps under Mirrorlake.

### Act 4: Secrets of the Old Blood (levels 8–11)
- **Goal:** wake Sylvenna, uncover the heroes' secret ties, and rescue the gifted from the Obsidian Spire.
- **Mirrorlake:** break the magisters' ice-seal with a **reflection puzzle**, angling mirrors to bring the sunlight down through the ice. Sylvenna tells the story of Malvoren the Night Wyrm.
- **Kingsgrave:** the crypts of House Vaelmont.
  - Doors only open for Vaelmont blood, and they open for **Marjohkan**. Reveal that he is the rightful king!
  - Find **Dawnfang** in the tomb of the last queen (a tomb puzzle: the statues' lineage order).
  - The Kingsgrave's secret passages run all the way into the palace.
- **Big twist for Magnus:** Sylvenna or Ysolde reveals that black scales mean night-dragon blood, and that the Long Night needs Magnus. The king's hunt for him becomes desperate.
- **The Obsidian Spire:** rescue the bottled magic and the captives. Final showdown with Krake. High Magister Grail lets them escape on purpose. Why? (He wants them to weaken the king for him.)

### Act 5: The Long Night (levels 11+)
- **Goal:** stop the ritual of the Long Night, break the Crown of Chains, and defeat the Night Wyrm.
- **The assault:** through the Kingsgrave secret passages (the Rat Roads) into Ravencrown palace, while the Lantern Society rises and freed dragons strike from the sky.
- **Throne room twist:** High Magister Grail betrays the king, grabs the crown, and tries to become the Night Wyrm himself. The finale is a three-way fight.
- **Final battle:**
  1. Malvoren as a giant shadow dragon in a darkening sky.
  2. Magnus must shatter the crown.
  3. Marjohkan must land Dawnfang.

## Hooks and side quests
- **The Haunted Mill (Dunmere):** the river mill grinds by itself at night. A friendly ghost miller hides a secret passage map of the Hearthlands. Reward: a rumour about the Kingsgrave.
- **Mother Gorse's Missing Griffin Chick (Lanternhold → Thornwood):** find it, and the heroes may keep a griffin-feather token or earn a pet discount.
- **The Dwarf Crown Puzzle (Stonehollow):** a riddle-lock vault that no dwarf has opened in 300 years. Inside is the Stonehollow armory.
- **The Dragon's Tooth:** a giant stone fang where the king "slew the last dragon". Climbing it reveals carvings of the truth, and a nest of storm-wyverns.
- **Pomfrey's Pamphlets:** the herald drops a sealed royal order in a tavern, which reveals the next Collector route.
- **Brannoc's Choice:** help Captain Brannoc's sick sister, and he becomes a secret ally inside the king's guard.
- **The Singing Statues (Ravencrown):** a city puzzle. Each of seven statues hums a note, and playing the right song opens a passage into the Rat Roads.

## Possible endings
- **Good:** the Crown of Chains shatters, the dragons are freed, and the Night Wyrm falls. Marjohkan is offered the throne. Does he take it, or give it to the people? Magnus is honoured by the scaled folk, and the dragons choose him as their friend.
- **Twisty:** Malvoren is defeated, but High Magister Grail escapes with a shard of the crown. Ysolde becomes the new, kind head of the magisters, and a sequel awaits.
- **Bad:** the Long Night falls. The sun vanishes over Valdmark, and the heroes must lead the resistance in a world of endless night (sequel campaign).

### Legendary: Dawnfang (Marjohkan)
- **Item:** hand, light. 1d6 + 3d6 radiant, Agility +2.
- **Special:** ignores the Armor and resistances of dragons and of anything wearing Chain-Iron.
- **Special:** glows near secret passages.
- **Lore:** the blade of Queen Elowen Vaelmont, forged with a dragon's blessing.
- **Growth:**
  - **Level 10:** "Royal Command": once per day, all non-boss enemies must make a Hard Wits roll or kneel (lose their action).
  - **Level 15:** "Dawn Strike": once per rest, a hit makes the sun break through: all allies heal 2d8.
  - **Level 20:** "True King": once per campaign, call every freed dragon to your side.

### Legendary: the Nightscale Mantle (Magnus)
- **Item:** body. Armor 2, Magic +3, Spell Power arcane +3, Resist shadow 8.
- **Special:** once per rest, take the shape of a black dragon for one round: fly, plus a breath attack of 4d6 arcane in an area.
- **Growth:**
  - **Level 15:** the dragon shape lasts a whole fight.
  - **Level 20:** "Starbreath": once per day, a breath of pure arcane starlight for 10d6 that ignores immunity.

## Design for the party
- **Marjohkan (rogue: silver tongue, scar, revenge):**
  - talking his way past guards
  - picking the abbey locks
  - unmasking Tobin with charm
  - the Kingsgrave doors opening for his blood
  - Dawnfang
  - the choice of revenge vs. mercy (Brannoc, Tobin, and maybe the king's songbird-nobles)
- **Magnus (arcane mage: black scales, serious, the hunted one):**
  - identifying magic items by touch
  - Arcane Missile shattering Chain-Iron locks (arcane hurts Chain-Iron)
  - his black scales calming the dragons
  - the Long Night twist
  - shattering the Crown of Chains
- **Pets:**
  - The glimmerkit is in Act 1, for whichever hero frees it.
  - The Menagerie in Lanternhold sells more pets.
  - The dragon egg is from Thornback. Ideally Magnus bonds with it; scales call to scales.
- **Riddles:** each act has at least one riddle and one puzzle. The Masked Owl's notes always come as riddles.
